## Intended Purpose

Draft clinical documentation aid for qualified healthcare professionals. Requires mandatory clinician review before patient record transfer.

<Warning>
**Critical Warning:** All AI-generated outputs require clinician verification. The system is NOT for autonomous clinical decision-making.
</Warning>

## Data Protection

Audio recordings and transcripts contain PHI/PII. Encrypted handling per HIPAA, GDPR, and UK GDPR is required.
